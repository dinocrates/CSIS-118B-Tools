Binary Fraction Builder

Convert an exact decimal fraction to binary by repeated doubling — one digit at a time, with exact rational arithmetic.

How to use this tool

  • Enter a decimal from 0 up to (but not including) 1, with up to six decimal places.
  • Next digit doubles the current remainder and records whether it crossed 1.
  • Some fractions terminate; others repeat forever. The tool detects both exactly, using integer arithmetic — never a rounded approximation.

Why some fractions terminate and others repeat

A reduced fraction terminates in base two if and only if its denominator is a power of two (a denominator of 1 — i.e. zero — also terminates). Fractions with any other prime factor in the denominator (such as 5, from tenths or fifths) repeat forever in binary, even though they are exact and finite in decimal.
