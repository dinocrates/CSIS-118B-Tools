Binary Addition with Carries

Add two fixed-width binary numbers one column at a time, tracking the carry as it moves left.

How to use this tool

  • Set the width and two operands, then predict the result before revealing it.
  • Next column processes one column (right to left) and shows its carry in, total, written bit, and carry out.
  • After all columns are revealed, the result summary explains carry-out and signed overflow — two different things.
