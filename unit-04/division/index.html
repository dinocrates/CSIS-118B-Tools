Division preparation lab

Prepare a 2×-width dividend (narrow zero/sign-extension, or a manual high:low pair) and divide with DIV or IDIV.

How to use this tool

  • Choose Narrow value to zero-extend (DIV/CBW-CWD-CDQ-CQO family) or sign-extend (IDIV) a single narrow value into the full dividend pair.
  • Choose Manual high:low to type both halves of the dividend pair directly as unsigned bit patterns — this is concatenation, not division.
  • A zero divisor or an out-of-range quotient is a Divide error: no quotient/remainder is produced, and any previous result is cleared.
